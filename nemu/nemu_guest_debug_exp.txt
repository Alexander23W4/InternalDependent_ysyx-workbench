How to debug guest program operation process in nemu:

----> QUICK VIEW:
使用gdb：
Display:    cpu.pc    paddr_read(cpu.pc, 4)   cpu.gpr[..]   
    display/x cpu.pc


----> ANALYSIS:
出于debug purpose oriented的底层思想， 直接在出问题的instr/pc或者是nemu-c内部的函数那里设置断点

-> 断instr和pc可以用watchpoint：
    watch (paddr_read(cpu.pc, 4) & 0x707f) == 0x2023   [watch all SW instr]
    watch paddr_read(cpu.pc, 4) == 0x02c787b3         [watch a specific instr]
    
    watch cpu.pc==0x80001258
    until cpu.pc == 0x80001258


-> 断c函数或者c语句直接用breakpoint, 可以用condition/if来限制(提升debug效率）：
     b mmio_read if cpu.pc == 0x80001258
     condition 1 cpu.pc == 0x80001258                  [add additional condition on triggering breakpoint 1]
