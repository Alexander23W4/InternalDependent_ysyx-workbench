BUS is a communication protocol between hardware modules

the module that initiates communication is generally called the master, and the module that responds to communication is called the slave.

--> Simplest BUS

In the simple interaction scenario above, the IFU, as the master, 
    sends information to the IDU, which is the slave, with the information being the current instruction.

Every cycle, the master sends valid information to the slave.
Once the master sends valid information, the slave can receive it immediately.

--> Asynchronous BUS 

Information is considered valid only when the valid signal is valid.
Once the master sends valid information, it is considered received by the slave immediately.

+-----+ inst  ---> +-----+
| IFU | valid ---> | IDU |
+-----+            +-----+

need to keep the processor's state unchanged when the instruction is invalid
it is necessary to add a ready signal to the communication content to indicate when the IDU can receive the next instruction.


Information is considered valid only when the valid signal is set.
The information sent by the master is considered received by the slave only when the ready signal is set.

--> Handshake Protocol

Both signals being set, also known as a "handshake," 
indicates that the master and slave have reached a consensus on the successful transmission of information.

The asynchronous bus determines when to communicate through a handshake protocol.

工作流： 需求 -> ISA -> 正确简单实现的 emulator & cpu & 驱动(for os) ->  通过pipeline, bus, cache提升硬件的能力

根据需求， 选用指令集和扩展。在software层面也要有所考量，因为应用都是基于software, 什么 VME IOE CTE，在ISA-CPU层实现所有的扩展指令功能。
后续在软件层面， 我们还需要开发底层驱动，让操作系统linux（在不同ISA与架构上实现抽象统一），能够调用的标准函数接口。 从而实现VME, CTE这样的基础软件功能。


用哪个指令集， emulator和cpu必须完整正确简单的实现这个指令集的所有功能， base on the manual
用difftest, trace等工具，debug, 确保基础功能不能够出错。
然后， 在cpu的功能上加上bus, trace, 外围设备的交互连接， 使用第三方hardware库辅助开发（verilog）
