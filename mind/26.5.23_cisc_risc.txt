this mind article is for the thought through RISC & CISC

The method to trade balance between different layers of system

->Function point of view:   (changable, flexible //  secure, general)
RISC is more flexible and light
for putting more task on software, make hardware simple and easy to develop and iterate

So, it's genuis to put complex thing as much as possible to the topper layer of the system, for it's flexibility, simple to operate and modify

Instead, put something need to be protected, & general, sustainable and solid functions to low layer of the system
and this should always includes consideration about necessity and simple-orientation


->Frequency point of view:  
put more frequently called things to lower layer

while less frequent, put into upper layer
